OVERVIEW

One platform, three people, one shared calendar.

DocPulse brings clinic administrators, doctors and patients together in one place. Administrators onboard staff, doctors publish the times they're available, and patients search, book and track their own appointments, without a single phone call.

Clinic Admin

Sets up the clinic

Invites doctors and staff by email, one at a time or in bulk, and manages who has access at any given time.

Doctor

Manages their schedule

Publishes their available hours and decides whether to accept, decline or complete each appointment request.

Patient

Books their own visit

Signs up in minutes, finds the right doctor and books, tracks or cancels an appointment whenever they need to.

PATIENT EXPERIENCE

From search to appointment, in a few clicks.

Booking a doctor's appointment should feel as easy as booking a table. Here's the journey a patient takes from the moment they hear about DocPulse to the moment they walk into an appointment.

1Creates their own account using just an email address, no admin approval needed to get started.
2Searches for a doctor by name or speciality, and filters by the date they'd like to be seen.
3Sees genuinely open slots, already updated so nothing double-booked or already elapsed is ever shown.
4Books in two clicks and receives an instant email confirmation of the request.
5Gets notified by email the moment the doctor confirms, declines or completes the visit, and can cancel any time beforehand.

RUNNING THE CLINIC

Doctors and admins stay firmly in control.

Doctors decide their own hours and their own patients. Admins decide who is allowed on the platform at all. Neither has to chase the other for updates.

Doctor

A schedule that works for them

Publishes blocks of available time in a few clicks, reviews incoming requests in one list, and confirms, declines or marks a visit complete. Years of experience can be kept up to date at any time.

Admin

Full oversight, minimal effort

Invites new doctors and staff by email, or uploads a spreadsheet to invite many at once. Sees the status of every invitation at a glance, and can revoke access immediately if needed.

Patients now sign themselves up. Admins are no longer needed to create a patient account, they simply invite doctors and other staff, reducing admin workload as the clinic grows.

BUILT TO BE DEPENDABLE

No double bookings. No requests left hanging.

A booking system is only as good as its guarantees. These are enforced at the deepest level of the platform, not just in the screens people see.

Guaranteed

Conflict-free scheduling

Two people can never end up booked into the same slot with the same doctor, even if they try at the exact same moment.

Automatic

Nothing falls through the cracks

A request a doctor hasn't responded to within 48 hours is automatically closed out, and the patient is told, so no one is left wondering.

Fair use

Sensible limits

Each patient can only hold a reasonable number of active requests at once, keeping the system fair for everyone using it.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Your data, protected by design.

Patient and clinic information is sensitive by nature. DocPulse is built with that in mind from the ground up.

Secure sign-in

Passwords are encrypted and session tokens are stored in a way that cannot be read or tampered with by the browser.

Private by default

Every person only ever sees their own appointments and records, never another patient's or doctor's.

Abuse protection

Automatic limits on how often the system can be used help prevent misuse, without affecting normal, everyday use.

Automatic confirmations

Every step, from invitation to booking to cancellation, is confirmed by email, so nothing depends on memory.
